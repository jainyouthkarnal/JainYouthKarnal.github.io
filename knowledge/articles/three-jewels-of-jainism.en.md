# The Three Jewels of Jainism

## Ratnatraya — The Three Jewels

**Ratnatraya** means the Three Jewels of Jainism: **Samyak Darshana (Right Faith), Samyak Gyan (Right Knowledge), and Samyak Charitra (Right Conduct).** Together, they form the path to spiritual liberation.

### 1. Samyak Darshana — Right Faith

Seeing and understanding reality with the right perspective and having faith in the true teachings.

### 2. Samyak Gyan — Right Knowledge

Gaining accurate knowledge of reality and understanding things as they truly are.

### 3. Samyak Charitra — Right Conduct

Putting right faith and right knowledge into practice through ethical living, self-discipline, and non-violence.

---

**The Three Jewels work together:** right faith guides our outlook, right knowledge deepens our understanding, and right conduct shapes how we live.
