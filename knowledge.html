SUNDAY PATHSHALA

Learn, grow & stay connected with Jain values.

Sunday Pathshala is a regular learning space for the Jain community in Karnal — bringing children and young learners together for Jain knowledge, values, stories, and sanskar.

EVERY SUNDAY 8:00 AM – 9:00 AM Jin Shasan Prabhavna Sangh — Karnal
01

Jain Dharma

Learn foundational Jain principles and core philosophical teachings.

02

Stories & Sanskar

Build connection with Jain values through stories, discussions, and reflection.

03

Community

Learn and grow together as part of the vibrant Jain Youth sangh in Karnal.
